Take

Already-rigged FBX: recognize the skeleton and build animator controls. Unrigged FBX: fit a starter skeleton and proximity skin to an upright Y-up humanoid mesh. Inspect the fit and weights before production use.

    Pose edit layer

    Click a joint, rotate it with the ring. The change blends in and out over the falloff so the capture detail stays.

    no joint

    Animation layers key Euler curves with Maya tangents and blend like Maya's layer stack. With a layer active, the ring keys into it instead of the pose layer.

    Animator rig

    Named body and finger controls key the existing animation. Hands and feet also offer a pose-preserving IK mode: switching modes does not move the skeleton; an IK key is created only when you apply a target.

    Contacts

    Shift-drag the timeline to pick frames, then lock a foot to the ground there.

        Support hand

        Captures the left hand's grip relative to the right hand at the current frame and holds it over the selected frames.

          Guided motion

          Range-limited generation preserves locked feet and hand/weapon constraints. Suggestions remain editable fixes.
