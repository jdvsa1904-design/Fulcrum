## Wiring order into app.js

Follow this order; each step lands only after its tests pass.

1. Filters before the FBX write, in `bakedClip` for every bone and frame: run `unrollQuats` / `eulerFilter` (Euler continuity in the FBX rotate order, XYZ) first, then `quatContinuity` (sign continuity, `dot >= 0`) on the quaternion tracks. Optional upstream cleanup runs before that and outside lock ranges: `peakRemoval*`, then Butterworth on root and pelvis (Fc 4-7 Hz). Leave the first and last frames and every frame inside a foot lock untouched.
2. `ik.js` replaces `twoBoneIK` in `app.js`. Use `stablePole` and `poleGuardTrack` for the legs in the sprint, plant, kneel and rise. Keep the previous frame's bend direction as state across the take.
3. Floor contact covers the kneel. Feed `floorContact` (feet) and `kneeFloor` (knee) into the IK goal as a lift (`liftOnPenetration`), run before the leg IK, with stiffness 0 on the plant and 0.5 to 1 on the slide, pivot Ankle on the plant. Plant ranges come from `detectPlants`, then `ankleLock`.
4. Gravity match covers the shot seams. `stitch.js` (Story Gravity match plus crossfade) runs on shot1, shot2 and shot3 before the contact and IK passes, so that the floor and lock logic sees the stitched timeline. The pipeline order inside a frame (stitch, then contact, then IK) is INFERRED from how HumanIK feeds floor contact into the reach step; the user-given order above is the order of landing, not of execution.
5. Re-run the FRT drill and check the 8 degree fidelity gate against the source capture. The unroll and sign steps change representation, not pose (INFERRED), so a gate failure there means a bug.
6. Export `exports/FRT_fulcrum_v3.fbx` and confirm the round-trip (last verified v2: 0.00 cm, 356 frames, 123 joints).
